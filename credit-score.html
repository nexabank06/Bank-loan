Understanding Credit Scores

Learn how credit scores work, what factors affect them, and how to improve yours to get better loan terms

What is a Credit Score?

A credit score is a 3-digit number (300-900) that shows your creditworthiness. It's based on your credit history, including:

  • ✓
    Payment History (35%)

    On-time payments of bills and loans

  • ✓
    Credit Utilization (30%)

    How much of your available credit you use

  • ✓
    Credit History Length (15%)

    How long you've had credit accounts

  • ✓
    Credit Mix (10%)

    Different types of credit (loans, cards)

  • ✓
    New Credit (10%)

    Recent credit inquiries and new accounts

Tips to Improve Your Credit Score

📅

Pay Bills on Time

Set up automatic payments or reminders to ensure you never miss a due date. This has the biggest impact on your score.

Impact: +35% of score

💳

Lower Credit Utilization

Try to use less than 30% of your available credit limit. Pay down balances regularly.

Impact: +30% of score

⏰

Keep Accounts Open

Don't close old credit cards. Longer credit history means higher scores.

Impact: +15% of score

🎯

Maintain Credit Mix

Have different types of credit: cards, loans, mortgages. Diversity helps your score.

Impact: +10% of score

🔍

Check for Errors

Review your credit report regularly for mistakes or fraud. Dispute errors immediately.

Impact: Variable

🚫

Avoid Hard Inquiries

Apply for credit sparingly. Multiple applications in a short time can lower your score.

Impact: +10% of score

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I check my credit score? ▼
Check your credit score at least quarterly to monitor changes. Many lenders provide free access. You can also get a free credit report from CIBIL once a year.
Does checking my credit score hurt it? ▼
No. Checking your own credit score is a "soft inquiry" and doesn't affect your score. Only hard inquiries from lenders impact it.
How long does it take to improve credit score? ▼
It typically takes 3-6 months to see improvements with responsible credit behavior. Major issues can take 2-3 years to fully resolve.
Can I get a loan with a poor credit score? ▼
Yes, but with higher interest rates and lower loan amounts. Consider improving your score first or looking for lenders specializing in poor credit.
What's the difference between CIBIL and other credit bureaus? ▼
CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, and CRIF are India's major credit bureaus. They use similar scoring models. Your score may vary slightly across bureaus based on available data.
